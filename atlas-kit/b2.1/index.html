What changed and why

Goal. Before any ATLAS number exists, check that the scorer itself is trustworthy: feed the ground-truth labels in as if they were a prediction (the oracle) and compare with an independent upper bound (the vertex-majority ceiling). A scorer that cannot score the truth near 1.0 cannot judge a method.

First attempt (job 990677) was invalid. It fed raw ground truth, including class -1, as the prediction. The vendored k-nearest-neighbour step silently drops -1 prediction points, so those surfaces took the labels of neighbouring scored classes and counted as false positives: the oracle scored 0.8905, with office_4 at 0.784, far below the ceiling 0.9455. This follows from reading the scorer code, and the fix makes the gate stricter, not looser.

Oracle v2 maps -1 to background (an ignored id) in the prediction, and was run once (job 993361, CPU, 1,116 s). Its validity test passed: the old construction run in the same job reproduced 0.890546568 exactly, with every per-scene value identical to 990677 within 1e-6.
