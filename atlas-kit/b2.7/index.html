b2.7: where does the ATLAS Replica signal die?

A series of room0 diagnostics: no bug in our pipeline; the released 24-d PCA basis discards the directions that separate Replica's classes, while raw 512-d features reach the band. Nothing here changes the frozen target.

01

What these diagnostics are

After the seed-0 miss, no band or target was touched. Instead a sequence of diagnostic-only jobs on room0 asked where the signal dies. Nothing here was recorded with the gate and no attempt was logged; the label b2.7 comes from the job names. Findings, in order:

  1. No bug in export, merge or label code (jobs 10452, 10473). Gaussian features equal the 2D frame features (norm 2.79 vs 2.79), neighbouring Gaussians agree (cosine 0.984 vs 0.43 for random pairs), the Gaussians match the projected 2D feature at cosine 0.968, and recomputing labels gives 0 differences.
  2. The 2D features themselves already give nonsense through the 24-d path: per-pixel argmax over 102 classes predicts only floor, book, logo and box.
  3. Resolution and aspect are not the lever (jobs 10602, 10658, 10974): six processing sizes, the 24-d path stays at or below 0.0003 3D mIoU everywhere.
  4. No load or decode bug (job 10658): the checkpoint loads as upstream does, round-trip cosine through the 24-d basis is 0.84.
  5. Raw 512-d features reach the band on room0: 3D mIoU 0.1197 at 320x240 (0.1355 at 640x480) against 0.0001 for the 24-d path.
  6. The released COCO-indoor basis spans only about 20 percent of the text direction (job 11189; the text vector's norm inside the 24-d subspace is 0.19 of 0.95): a genuine loss in ATLAS's released compression on Replica's 102-class vocabulary under our protocol, not an install fault.
Scope

All diagnostics are room0 only, single seed, not gated. Whether the paper scored Replica from a basis fitted on the scene (ATLAS ships a fitting routine) or from 512-d features is an open protocol question; the pre-registered rule did not allow switching after the fact.

HM3D memory, seeds 1 and 2 (in progress)

The six extra memory runs must share one GPU model. The first attempt (10737) completed the 2 m and 5 m runs on an L40S (13.14 and 10.37 GiB) and then hung in the container's final teardown; a teardown watchdog was added (acting only after the measurement) and a resume job (13128) re-runs all six on a single RTX 6000 Ada, so the L40S numbers are superseded and reported only. At the time of writing, the 13128 2 m seed-1 run reports 13.17 GiB and the 5 m run ended with return code 5; the job is not finished and nothing is interpreted.
