What this version did, and why

Goal. Evaluate the released checkpoint at the Isaac-GR00T release commit with the README's flags, 100 episodes per seed, seeds 0, 1 and 2.

What happened. Seed 0 completed and passed: 62/100 = 0.62, inside the band [0.43, 0.73], native print equal to the wrapper SUMMARY. Seeds 1 and 2 were submitted on a shared 64-core A40 node with --mem 64G and were killed by the kernel for host RAM (a vector-env worker pipe broke, client rc=1) at 76/100 and 71/100 episodes, after about 45 minutes of rollout. They produced no SUMMARY line, so there was no result to record or to drop. The cure is a resource change only, described in b1.2.
