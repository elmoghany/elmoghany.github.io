What changed and why

With the build fixed, the other seven Replica scenes were run (one job each, jobs 1301 to 1370) and one CPU job scored all eight (job 2238). The anchor passed. ConceptGraphs ViT-H-14, scored by our scorer, lands inside the published band on all three metrics, which validates the scorer and the Replica protocol that ATLAS will be judged on.
