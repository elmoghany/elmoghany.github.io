01

What changed and why

Change. Seeds 1 and 2 were rerun with exactly one resource change: SLURM --mem 64G to --mem 160G, plus a measurement-only host-RAM sampler in run_seed.sh (a background loop that logs the job cgroup's memory.current and the top-RSS processes every 60 s).

Why. The two b1.1 reruns died of host-RAM exhaustion, not of any evaluation problem. The sampler confirms the diagnosis: memory grows steadily during rollout and exceeds 64 GB.
